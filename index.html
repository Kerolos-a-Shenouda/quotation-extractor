Reads any supplier's quotation with a layout-aware engine: it rebuilds the table columns from the PDF, finds each line item by checking Qty × Unit Price = Line Total, applies line discounts, and then verifies the sum of all lines against the quotation's own subtotal. Rows or files that don't add up are flagged for review instead of being silently wrong. Supplier-specific rules for Krones, KHS, Alhuwais (SKF), ARCOMA (Festo), Tetra Pak, GEA, Surdry, Lan Handling, Netstal, Kieselmann, Atlas Copco, Parker (Addamegh) and Veolia still run as a cross-check, and Festo part numbers are decoded into shape / thread / tube size / material. Scanned PDFs and photos are read with OCR (first scan needs an internet connection to load the OCR engine). Extracted lines can be sent straight to the Spare Parts grid in Power Apps (Supplier PN, Other Description, Inventory unit and Vendor Name).
